Join us

An open group: you do not need to be at NTU to take part.

Who

Students and researchers who work on large language models or on wireless communications, and who want to test what language models can and cannot do on real engineering problems. You do not need both backgrounds, and you do not need to be at NTU: the group is open to people at any university or institute.

How to start

  1. Write to contact@openmaxwell.org with a few lines on what you would like to work on and anything you have built before.
  2. Read one of our papers closely, ideally the one nearest your interest, and come with a question it leaves open.
  3. Start with a scoped piece of an existing project before leading your own.

How projects run

There is no fixed meeting schedule. Each project meets when the work needs it, and the people on it agree how often.

Authorship

  • Authorship follows substantive contribution. An author has contributed to the idea, the data, the experiments or the analysis, has helped write or revise the paper, and can answer for the work, in the spirit of the ICMJE criteria; individual contributions are recorded with the CRediT roles.
  • Author order is agreed when a project starts. If contributions change, any change to the order is discussed openly and in advance.
  • People who contribute to a dataset or benchmark without meeting these criteria are named in its acknowledgements or contributor list.
