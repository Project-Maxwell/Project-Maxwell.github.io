We use wireless communications as a testbed for large language models and agents: its problems come with derivations, simulators and measurements, so answers can be checked against physics.

An open research community for wireless and LLMs, started in 2025 at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, and open to people at any institution.

02

Perception

Can a model infer the radio field a city shapes from what an image or a few measurements show?

RadioVISTA

Preprint

Seeing the Invisible: How Vision-Language Models Read, Predict and Trace Radio Fields

Yiyang Zhu, Xin Li, Guangyuan Liu, Ziheng Liu, Jiayi Zhang, Chau Yuen

A benchmark and training dataset for visual reasoning about invisible radio fields, built by an engine that turns any coordinate on Earth into a ray-traceable city.

Project

Radio2Map

Preprint

Reading 3D Cities from Sparse Radio Measurements with an Image Editor

Xin Li, Yiyang Zhu, Chau Yuen

Reconstructs a city's 3D geometry from a sparse set of radio measurements, using an image-editing model. The paper and its figures go public with the arXiv release.

03

Agents

How should agents exchange messages and tasks across a network, and what has to be standard?

LACP

AI4NextG @ NeurIPS 2025

LLM Agent Communication Protocol (LACP) Requires Urgent Standardization: A Telecom-Inspired Protocol is Necessary

Xin Li, Mengbing Liu, Chau Yuen

A position paper: LLM agents need one telecom-style communication standard before today's ad-hoc protocols entrench. LACP has three layers (semantic, transactional, transport) with authentication and transactional integrity in the core.

Join us

Students and researchers who work on large language models or on wireless communications, and who want to test what language models can and cannot do on real engineering problems. You do not need both backgrounds, and you do not need to be at NTU: the group is open to people at any university or institute.
